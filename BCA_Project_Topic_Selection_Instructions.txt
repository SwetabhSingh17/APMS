4. TEAM VERIFICATION (IMPORTANT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Project teams (2 to 5 members) have already been mapped and allotted in 
   the portal as per official records.
2. After logging in, verify your assigned group members under the "My Project Team" 
   section.
3. Every team can choose ONLY ONE project topic.
4. When any one authorized team member confirms a topic selection, that topic is 
   automatically allotted to the ENTIRE team.
5. All team members MUST discuss, coordinate, and agree on their preferred 
   topics beforehand to avoid conflicting choices.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STEP-BY-STEP TOPIC SELECTION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow these steps to select your project:

Step 1: Open the Portal and Log In
  - Go to the portal URL in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
  - Enter your Enrollment Number and Password.

Step 2: Navigate to "Project Topics"
  - In the left sidebar navigation menu, click on "Project Topics".
  - You will see the complete catalog of faculty-approved project topics.

Step 3: Browse, Search, and Filter Topics
  - Search Bar: Search by project title, technology stack (e.g., Python, MERN, 
    Flutter, Machine Learning, IoT), or PUGID code (e.g., PUGID26001).
  - Category Tabs:
      * "All Topics": Complete list of topics.
      * "Available": Topics that are currently open for selection (Green indicator).
      * "Unavailable": Topics already claimed/selected by other teams (Red indicator).

Step 4: Review Topic Details
  - Click on any topic card to review the complete description, faculty supervisor, 
    prerequisites, and required technologies.

Step 5: Select & Confirm Your Topic
  - Once your team has agreed on an available topic, click the "Select Topic" button.
  - A confirmation popup dialog will appear displaying the project title.
  - Carefully verify the details, then click "Confirm".
  - A green confirmation notification will appear: "Topic selected successfully".

Step 6: Verify in "Projects" Section
  - The chosen topic will now be locked for your team and featured at the top 
    of your "Project Topics" page.
  - You can also view your active project and future milestone submissions in 
    the "Projects" menu (`/projects`).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CRITICAL RULES & DEADLINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• FIRST-COME, FIRST-SERVED: Topic allotment is strictly on a first-come, 
  first-served basis. Once a topic is confirmed by a team, it becomes unavailable 
  to all other teams immediately.
• SELECTION IS FINAL: Topic selection cannot be changed, reverted, or transferred 
  by students once submitted. Do not make hasty selections without team agreement.
• SINGLE SUBMISSION PER TEAM: Only one member needs to submit the selection on 
  behalf of the entire team.
